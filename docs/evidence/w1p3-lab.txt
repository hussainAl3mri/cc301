3. Question: In the real cloud, who might delete a resource without telling anyone, and why would plan save you?

Answer:
A team member or cloud administrator might accidentally delete a resource manually without informing the team. Terraform plan detects that the resource is missing and shows what needs to be recreated. This helps us identify the problem before making changes.
